Privacy Policy

Routinee respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it is stored, and what rights you have. By using Routinee, you agree to these practices.

Last updated: June 2025

1. Data we collect

We may collect your name, email address, account credentials, financial data such as expenses and budgets, productivity data such as habits, tasks, routines, reminder preferences, and special day entries.

2. How data is used

Your data is used to provide app functionality, enable secure syncing, improve reliability, process notifications, and support customer care. We do not sell your personal data to third parties for advertising or marketing.

3. Storage and security

Routinee uses Appwrite Cloud as its backend for secure data storage, authentication, and account management. We apply reasonable safeguards, but no service can guarantee absolute protection.

4. Your rights

  • Request access to your data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and related records
  • Export your data where feasible

5. Third-party services

Routinee uses Google Play for distribution and Appwrite for cloud services. These providers process data only as necessary to provide their services and are subject to their own legal terms.

6. Children’s privacy

Routinee is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
