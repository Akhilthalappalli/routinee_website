Account & data

Delete your Routinee account.

You're always in control of your data. Here's exactly how to delete your account, what gets removed, and how long it takes.

Delete in the app No app access? What gets deleted Retention period Shared partner data Contact

1. Delete your account from within the app

  1. Open the Routinee app and sign in
  2. Go to Settings → Account
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Your account and data are removed immediately — there's no waiting period once you confirm in-app.

2. Don't have access to the app anymore?

If you've lost access to your device or can no longer sign in, email us at routineeapp@gmail.com from the address linked to your account and request deletion. We'll verify your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days.

3. What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Profile — your name, email, login credentials, and account settings
  • Expenses & budgets — all logged expenses, categories, and budget limits
  • Habits — habit definitions, streaks, and habit logs
  • Tasks — open and completed tasks
  • Savings — savings goals and progress entries
  • Couple data — shared budgets, todos, love notes, gifts, mood check-ins, special days, shared goals, memories, and journey entries you created
  • Photos — any images uploaded to the Memory Journal / Relationship Map
  • Device data — your push notification token and onboarding answers

4. Retention period

When you delete your account from within the app, deletion is immediate — your data is removed from our active systems right away. If you request deletion by email instead, we complete it within 30 days of verifying your request.

5. Shared partner data

If you were connected to a partner, deleting your account also disconnects the partnership and removes your content from shared couple features. Content your partner created independently (their own expenses, notes, etc.) remains in their account.

6. Contact

Questions about account deletion or data removal: routineeapp@gmail.com.

Need help first?

Not sure you want to delete everything?

You can also export your data, manage notifications, or reach out with questions before taking this step.
